China's market regulator has reportedly cleared Savvy Games Group's purchase of Moonton, the studio behind Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. No conditions attached. The deal hasn't closed yet.

ByteDance paid about $4B for Moonton in 2021. This deal is valued at more than $6B.

With Scopely ($4.9B, agreed in 2023), that's more than $10.9B on two mobile games companies.

In both deals the existing management stays in charge. Moonton's CEO Zhang Yunfan stays on.

What I'll watch: the closing announcement.

If you're building mobile games in the region, would you rather sell one day, or stay independent?
