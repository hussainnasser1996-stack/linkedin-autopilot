China's market regulator has reportedly cleared Savvy Games Group's purchase of Moonton, the studio behind Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. No conditions attached.

The review was completed on 29 October and the notice went up on 6 November. The deal hasn't closed yet.

Two things caught my eye:

1. The price. The deal, agreed in March, is valued at more than $6B. ByteDance paid about $4B for Moonton in 2021, so it reportedly makes about $2B on the sale.

2. The scale. Savvy agreed to pay $4.9B for Scopely in 2023. With Moonton, that's more than $10.9B on two mobile games companies.

Moonton keeps its management, and CEO Zhang Yunfan stays on. Scopely was also set up to run as an autonomous company under Savvy. So the pattern is big mobile companies, bought with the existing teams left in charge.

What I'll watch: the closing announcement, and what Savvy says about Moonton's plans after it.

If you're building mobile games in the region, would you rather sell to a buyer like this one day, or stay independent?
