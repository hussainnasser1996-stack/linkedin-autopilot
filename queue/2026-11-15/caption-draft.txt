China's regulator has cleared a $6B Saudi games deal — and it's not just a formality.

Savvy Games Group's purchase of Moonton, the studio behind Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, was reportedly approved unconditionally by China's market regulator. The review was completed on 29 October; the notice went up on 6 November.

Here's what stands out:

1. The price. The deal is valued at more than $6B, agreed in March. ByteDance paid about $4B for Moonton in 2021, so the seller reportedly walks away with about $2B of profit.

2. The approval. No conditions. A Saudi buyer taking over a major Shanghai studio cleared review in one step.

3. The scale. Savvy agreed to pay $4.9B for Scopely in 2023. Add Moonton and that's more than $10.9B on two mobile game companies.

Moonton keeps its management, and CEO Zhang Yunfan stays. The deal hasn't closed yet.

That's the real signal for studios in the region.

What I'll watch: the closing announcement, and what Savvy says about Moonton's plans.
