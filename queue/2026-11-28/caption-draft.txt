Dell booked $60.2bn of AI server orders last quarter — and recognised $22.0bn as revenue.
The result: its AI server backlog hit a record $132.3bn, up from $95bn three months earlier.
At Q3's pace, that's about six quarters of work. Dell says demand still runs ahead of component supply.
For GCC compute buyers, that queue is the real lead time.
Sources: Dell Q3 FY27 earnings call, Dell Q2 FY27 results
