Acwa's Q3 net profit jumped 71%. Over nine months it's up 0.6%.

I'd read the second number first. The slides show why.

Q3 2026, from the Tadawul filing on 5 November:
Net profit: SAR 635.1M, up 71.1%
Revenue: SAR 2.2B, up 32.7%

Nine months:
Revenue: SAR 6.24B, up 16%
Net profit: SAR 1.29B, up 0.6%
Operating income: SAR 2.55B, down 8.8%

Two things stood out:

1. Timing. Acwa books development fees when projects hit milestones, and the company says milestones slipped this year. That can swing a single quarter a long way.

2. The build. Acwa has 29 projects (46.2 GW) under construction and raised SAR 30B of non-recourse funding this year. Earnings will depend on when those plants switch on, and at what funding cost.

What I'll watch: Q4 milestones and full-year operating income.

When you read a developer like this, do you start with the quarter or the year to date?

Sources: Acwa; Tadawul filing via Arab News; Enterprise
