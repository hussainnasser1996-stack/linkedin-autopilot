Isomorphic Labs has raised $5bn. It still has no drug in clinical trials.

The AI drug discovery company raised $2.1bn in May. Six months later, $5bn more. That's $7.1bn in one year, at a valuation reported at about $50bn.

Two things:

1. Investors are paying for the platform before any human data. The new money is meant to take its first programmes into trials.

2. Gulf capital is in it. Abu Dhabi's MGX came back for this round. Greenoaks and Sequoia co-led.

For GCC life-science funds, it's the same question at a smaller size: how much do you pay for a platform before the first patient?

What would you need to see before backing an AI drug company with no clinical data yet?
