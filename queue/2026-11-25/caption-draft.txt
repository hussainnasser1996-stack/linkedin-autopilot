Isomorphic Labs just raised $5bn — and it still has no drug in clinical trials.

That's not a weakness, it's the whole bet.

The AI drug-discovery company raised $2.1bn in May. Six months later, $5bn more. That's $7.1bn in one year, at a valuation reported around $50bn.

Here's what stands out:

1. Investors are paying for the platform before any human data. The money is aimed at getting its first programmes into trials.

2. Gulf capital is in it. Abu Dhabi's MGX is a returning backer, alongside Thrive, GV and Temasek. Greenoaks and Sequoia co-led.

For GCC life-science funds, it's a pivotal test case: how much do you pay for a platform before the first patient?
