## Caption

TSMC sold NT$586.9B of chips in October. A monthly record.

That's up 59.7% on October 2025 (NT$367.5B) and 14.7% on September. The previous record was August, at NT$514.8B.

January to October: NT$4,485.6B, up 43.3%.

Why I'm reading it today: Nvidia reports its quarter later today, US time. TSMC's monthly sales come out first, so I treat them as the earliest public read on chip supply. That matters for anyone buying GPUs, including the AI data centres being built in the Gulf.

Two things I'll watch:
1. Whether Nvidia's numbers show the same jump.
2. What it says about supply into 2027.

If you're planning compute in the region, which do you check first: chip supply or power?
