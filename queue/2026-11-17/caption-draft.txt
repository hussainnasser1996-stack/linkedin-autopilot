TSMC just posted its biggest month ever — and it's not just a chip story, it's a Gulf compute story.

October revenue: NT$586.9B. Up 59.7% on October 2025 and 14.7% on September. The previous record was August, at NT$514.8B.

January to October: NT$4,485.6B, up 43.3%.

Here's why it matters today: Nvidia reports its quarter later today, US time. TSMC's monthly sales come out first, making them the earliest public read on chip supply, a crucial signal for anyone buying GPUs, including the AI data centres being built in the Gulf.

Two things I'll watch tonight:
1. Whether Nvidia's numbers show the same jump.
2. What it says about supply into 2027.

The signal is clear.

If you're planning compute in the region, which number do you check first: chip supply or power?
