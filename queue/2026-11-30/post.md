## Caption
Oman's sovereign wealth fund has joined a nearly $200m round for a company whose robots run lab experiments for AI. The slides show what it does.

MegaRobo, a Beijing lab-automation company, announced the round on 19 Nov:
Round: nearly $200m
Raised so far: about $600m, the company says
Customers: more than 500, mostly drug makers, biotech firms and research labs

Two things:

1. The product is the lab. An AI plans a test, robots run it, and the result picks the next test.

2. It plans to expand into the Middle East, among other regions.

On 25 Nov I wrote about $5bn going into an AI drug company before any trial data. Platforms like that need results from real experiments, and MegaRobo sells the labs that produce them.

If you work in a lab here, would you send experiments to a robot lab or build your own?

Sources: The Next Web; Oman Observer (19 Nov 2026)
