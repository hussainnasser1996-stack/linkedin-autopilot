Oman's sovereign fund just joined a nearly $200m round for a company whose robots run lab experiments for AI. The slides show what it sells.

MegaRobo, based in Beijing, announced the round on 19 Nov:
Round: nearly $200m
Raised in total: about $600m, by its own count
Customers: more than 500, mostly drug makers, biotech firms and research labs

Two things:

1. It sells the lab, not the model. An AI plans a test, robots run it, and the result picks the next test. With Tencent Cloud and Twist Bioscience, it will test more than 10,000 AI-designed proteins.

2. The Middle East is on its expansion list. Gulf money is now backing the physical side of AI for science, not only the models.

For GCC research hubs, the real bottleneck may be lab capacity more than algorithms.

If you run a lab here, would you send experiments to a robot lab abroad, or build one?
