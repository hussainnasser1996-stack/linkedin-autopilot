## Caption

Saudi startups raised $1B in the first nine months of 2026. Two deals made up 56% of it.

MAGNiTT's 9M figures, against the same period last year:

Funding: down 29%
Deals: 113, down 39%
Funding outside the two mega rounds: down 50%
Active investors: down 26%
International investor participation: down 51%

Two things I'd take from it:

1. Two rounds worth $563M carry the headline. Take them out and the rest of the market is back at 9M 2024 levels.

2. International investors pulled back the most. That leaves more of the work to local funds, and more depends on how steadily they deploy.

Series A held up better than the rest. Deal count there fell 17%, so companies with traction are still raising. Early-stage activity is where it softened.

For the next quarter I'll watch non-mega funding before the headline total.
