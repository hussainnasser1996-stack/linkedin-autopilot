Silver Lake and PIF reportedly discussed buying Unity at about $25B. No deal has been agreed.

After EA, it's not just another games deal — it's a move from owning games to owning the tools studios use.

Here's what the buyer has to believe at $25B:

1. The price: about 16% above Unity's roughly $21.5B market cap after Q3. Q3 adjusted EBITDA was $257M (41% margin). Annualised, $25B is about 24 times that.

2. The cash: Q3 free cash flow was $220M. Run-rate, that's about a 3.5% yield on $25B. That only works if growth keeps going.

3. The growth: Grow Solutions, the ads business, rose 44% to $457M. Create, the engine, went to $165M from $152M. Q4 guidance is $290M–$295M of adjusted EBITDA, which would take the multiple to about 21 times.

So the bet is on the ad network, not the engine.

What I'll watch: whether Unity confirms a sale process, and the price per share if it does.

Deal people here: at roughly 24 times EBITDA, would you underwrite it?
