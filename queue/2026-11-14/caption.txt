Silver Lake and PIF reportedly discussed buying Unity at about $25B. No deal has been agreed.

That's about 16% above Unity's market cap, and about 24 times its annualised Q3 adjusted EBITDA of $257M.

Two things stand out in Unity's Q3:

1. Ads drive the growth. Grow Solutions rose 44% to $457M. Create, the engine, went to $165M from $152M.

2. Cash. Free cash flow was $220M. On a run-rate, that's about a 3.5% yield at $25B.

What I'll watch: whether Unity confirms a sale process.

Studio founders: do you see Unity more as an engine or an ads business?
