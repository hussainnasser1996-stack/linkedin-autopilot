## Caption
Nvidia kept a 74.8% gross margin last quarter. For next quarter it guides 71% to 72%. The slides show the step down.

The quarter to 25 October, from Nvidia's release:
Revenue: $115.8bn, up 103%
Data centre: $107.6bn, up 110%
Gross margin: 74.8%, against a 74.0% guide

Two things I'd look at:

1. The Q4 guide is $130bn of revenue at a 71% to 72% margin. Q4 has one extra week, so the revenue jump flatters a little.

2. On the call, the CFO said Q4 should be the low point, with 72% to 73% expected in fiscal 2028.

On 13 Nov I wrote that the chip layer keeps most of the AI data-centre margin. About 3 points lower, it still does. For Gulf buyers of compute, that margin sits inside every GPU price.

If you buy or resell compute here, do you track Nvidia's margin in your planning?
