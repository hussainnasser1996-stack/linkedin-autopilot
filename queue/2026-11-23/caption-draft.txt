Nvidia kept 74.8% gross margin last quarter. Next quarter it guides 71% to 72%. Swipe to see why that matters.

The quarter to 25 October, from Nvidia's release:
Revenue: $115.8bn, up 103%
Data centre: $107.6bn, up 110%
Gross margin: 74.8%, against a 74.0% guide

Two things I'd look at:

1. The real story isn't the revenue — it's the margin. Q4 guide: $130bn at 71% to 72%. Q4 also has an extra week.

2. On the call, the CFO said Q4 should be the bottom, with 72% to 73% in fiscal 2028.

On 13 Nov I wrote that the chip layer keeps most of the AI data-centre margin. About 3 points lower, it still does.

That matters.

For Gulf buyers of compute, that margin is a crucial part of every GPU price.

If you buy or resell compute in the Gulf, do you track Nvidia's margin in your planning?
