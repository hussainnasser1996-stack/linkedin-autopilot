Saudi savers now hold more in time and savings deposits than in current accounts — the first time since 2008. And that's not just a statistic, it's a funding story. Swipe to see why it matters.

End of September, from SAMA data:
Time and savings: SAR 1.417tn, 46.1% of deposits
Demand: SAR 1.405tn, 45.7%
A year ago: 39.3% against 50.4%.

Two key things I'd watch:

1. Time deposits pay interest. Current accounts mostly don't. So the same deposit base now costs banks more to fund.

2. How much of that reaches margins. Asharq Al-Awsat notes a higher share doesn't have to raise funding costs one for one. Q4 results will show it.

That's the crucial number for Q4.

Bank treasurers and analysts here: are you seeing it in your cost of funds?

Sources: SAMA data via Asharq Al-Awsat and Argaam
