## Caption
Emaar is offering AED 15 a share for the part of Emaar Development it doesn't own. Is that a 3% premium or a 149% premium?

Both. It depends on which start date you pick. The slides show all five.

From Emaar's offer page:
vs the 2017 subscription price (AED 6.03): +148.8%
vs the 1-week average price: +21.7%
vs the 2 Nov close: +19.4%
vs the 3-month average price: +15.7%
vs the 5 Nov close: +3.3%

Two things I'd look at:

1. 2 Nov was the last trading day before the board announcement. I'd measure from there: 19.4%.

2. After the news the share closed at AED 14.52 on 5 Nov. From that price the offer adds 3.3%.

Emaar already holds just over 79%. The offer is open until 10 December.

Which price do you use to judge a buyout premium?
