## Caption

Nearly half of WeRide's revenue now comes from outside China.

Q3 2026 revenue rose 88.0% to RMB321.5m. Overseas was RMB146.9m, up 171.5%, or 45.7% of the total.

Two things I noticed in the results:

1. Gross margin rose to 43.0% from 32.9% a year ago. WeRide calls overseas its higher-margin business, and more than 90% of its robotaxi revenue now comes from there.

2. The loss barely moved. RMB310.0m this quarter, against RMB307.3m a year ago, while revenue grew 88%.

Its Middle East fleet is about 600 vehicles, up from about 400 at the end of July. The target is full coverage of core Abu Dhabi and Dubai by year-end.

If you look at robotaxis here, what would you watch next: margin per car, or the loss line?

Sources: WeRide Q3 2026 results (20 Nov 2026)
