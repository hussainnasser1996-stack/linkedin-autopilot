WeRide now makes nearly half its revenue outside China — and the Gulf is a big part of why.

Q3 2026 revenue rose 88.0% to RMB321.5m. Overseas revenue was RMB146.9m, up 171.5%, or 45.7% of the total.

This isn't just a pilot story anymore. It's a revenue story.

Two things stand out:

1. Gross margin rose to 43.0% from 32.9% a year ago. WeRide calls overseas its higher-margin business, and more than 90% of its robotaxi revenue now comes from there.

2. The loss hasn't moved. RMB310.0m this quarter, against RMB307.3m a year ago, while revenue nearly doubled.

The Middle East fleet is about 600 vehicles, with full coverage of core Abu Dhabi and Dubai targeted by year-end.

That's a pivotal shift for the region.

For investors here, what would you watch next: margin per car, or the loss line?
