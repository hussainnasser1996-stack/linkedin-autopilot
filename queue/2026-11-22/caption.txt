Nearly 200 of Uber's driverless cars will be on UAE roads by year-end.

Uber expects about 700 worldwide by then, so that's more than a quarter of the fleet in one country.

Two things I noticed in Uber's Q3 remarks:

1. The plan is over 2,000 driverless vehicles by the end of 2027, roughly half in the US and half outside it.

2. Uber says its focus is moving from partnerships and testing to driverless commercial service. The next test is how much each car is used and what it earns.

For Gulf investors, I think this is now a fleet business. Someone has to buy the cars, finance them, keep them on the road and run the depots. That's where I'd look for local companies to build.

If you work in mobility here, which part of the fleet would you want to own?

Sources: Uber Q3 2026 prepared remarks and earnings call (4 Nov 2026)
