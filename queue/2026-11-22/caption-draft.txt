Uber expects about 700 fully driverless cars on its app by the end of 2026. Nearly 200 of them will be in the UAE — not just a pilot, but a core market.

This isn't a small test anymore. It's a pivotal moment for the region.

Two things stand out:
1. The target is over 2,000 driverless vehicles by end-2027, roughly half in the US and half internationally.
2. Uber says the focus now is utilisation and unit economics, not partnerships and testing.

For GCC operators, fleet owners and city planners, the question shifts from "will it come" to who runs the fleet, who finances the cars and who keeps the margin.
