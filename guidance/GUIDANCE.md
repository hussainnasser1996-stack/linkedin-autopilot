# Content guidance — Hussain Nasser's LinkedIn (edit freely; the routine reads this every run)

## Goal
Reputation, not reach. Posts compound into thought leadership that earns senior roles,
partnerships and board/advisory seats in Saudi/GCC venture, AI and games.
Not lead-gen. Never sound like he is job-hunting.

## Who he is (public framing only)
- "I work across venture building, AI and games in Saudi Arabia."
- Portfolio Manager, Revival Lab (venture builder) · Investment Director, a global AI fund · FMVA®.
- Based between Bahrain and KSA; audience is Saudi/GCC investors, founders, operators, gov-adjacent.

## Content pillars (rotate; no pillar twice in a row)
1. **Saudi results, in motion** — listed Saudi/GCC company results read like an investor (Aramco, SNB, STC, Ma'aden, ACWA, Al Rajhi…). Bars card with real reported figures.
2. **Gulf capital × games** — Saudi/GCC game studio rounds, Savvy/PIF/Scopely/EA, Merak, Impact46, Qiddiya, esports economics, the after-seed gap.
3. **Gulf VC & venture building** — MAGNiTT/Wamda/Dealroom data, fund launches, deployment discipline, LP behaviour, venture-studio model.
4. **AI in the Gulf** — HUMAIN, sovereign AI compute, data centres, applied AI adoption in KSA; global AI deals that matter for the region.
5. **Builder's notes / reflections** — short, opinionated lessons from operating and investing (no confidential details).

## Weekly rhythm (KSA time; posts publish 09:00 Asia/Riyadh)
- One post every day, 7 days a week (about 30 a month).
- Each week: at least one bars/data card, at most two reflections.

## Research rules
- Only use news from the last 14 days (results/data can be from the latest reporting period).
- Every number on the card or in the caption needs a named, linkable source; keep the links in the draft file.
- Prefer primary sources (company filings, Tadawul, SAMA, GASTAT, PIF, MAGNiTT, official press releases), then
  Reuters/Bloomberg/Argaam/Arab News/Asharq/Wamda/PocketGamer.biz/Dealroom.
- If a figure can't be confirmed in two places (or one primary), drop it or phrase it as "reported".
- Say "I couldn't find" rather than "there is no".

## Hard rules (never break)
- Never mention CFA (FMVA is fine). No Arabic name or transliterations.
- No Singular Link or Revival Lab figures, NAVs, valuations, deal terms, portfolio company names, or internal details.
  Never mention General Intuition. Never claim realised exits.
- No job-search wording, no "open to opportunities", no coffee/meeting asks.
- Don't call himself "investor" as an identity line; don't write "based in Riyadh".
- Don't lecture senior people or claim insider knowledge. Confident on public data, humble on judgement.
- No politics, religion, or criticism of Saudi government bodies. Constructive framing on Vision 2030.
- No emojis. No "leverage/synergy/unlock value". No hype ("game-changer", "revolutionary").

## Post format
- 120–220 words. Hook in line 1 (a number or a sharp claim). Short paragraphs, many line breaks.
- Numbered points when listing ("Two things: 1… 2…").
- End with one honest question to the reader.
- Sources line just above the hashtags: "Sources: …" (names, not URLs unless short). No first comment (Buffer Free plan).
- Hashtags: 5–6 on the last line. Core: #VentureCapital #GCC #SaudiArabia; add 2–3 topic tags (#Gaming #GameDev #AI #Vision2030 #VentureBuilding …).
- Tag at most 2 company pages, only ones named in the post, and only positive/neutral mentions.

## Card
- Types: `bars` (data), `statement` (one sharp line), `compare` (two columns). See examples/.
- Eyebrow = the pillar name in short (e.g. "Saudi results", "Gulf capital × games", "Market take").
- Card text must agree with the caption figures exactly.
