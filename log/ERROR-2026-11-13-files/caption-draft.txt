Acwa's Q3 net profit jumped 71%. Over nine months it's up 0.6%.

Both numbers are true. The slides show which one I'd read first.

Q3 2026, from the Tadawul filing on 5 November:
Net profit: SAR 635.1M, up 71.1%
Revenue: SAR 2.2B, up 32.7%

Nine months:
Net profit: SAR 1.29B, up 0.6%
Operating income: SAR 2.55B, down 8.8%

Two things stood out:

1. Timing. Acwa books development fees when projects hit milestones, and the company says milestones slipped this year. That timing can swing a single quarter a long way.

2. The build. 29 projects, 46.2 GW, are under construction, and Acwa raised SAR 30B of non-recourse funding this year. What earnings look like depends on when those plants switch on, and at what funding cost.

What I'll watch: Q4 milestones and full-year operating income.

When you read a developer like this, do you start with the quarter or the year to date?
