# Anthropic's debut, a UK wealth buy and $5bn for AI drug discovery

Gulf money was busy abroad between 15 and 22 November. Here are three deals, plus the dates I'm watching next week.

## The week in Gulf deals

### 1. Anthropic trades, and its Gulf backers get a market price

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share and raised $81.3 billion, the largest IPO on record. On its first day on Nasdaq, 19 November, the stock opened at $111.50.

The figure: it closed at $97.17, 17% above the IPO price, on a day the Nasdaq fell 2.2%.

Why it matters: QIA invested in the Series F and MGX co-led the Series G, both before the listing. Their stakes now have a daily public price.

### 2. Mubadala buys into UK wealth management

Mubadala and Centerbridge agreed to buy Canaccord Genuity's UK and Crown Dependencies wealth business in an all-cash deal. Management keeps a stake, and completion is expected in the first half of 2027.

The figure: an enterprise value of about £1.17 billion, for £43.4 billion of client assets. By my maths, that's roughly 2.7% of assets.

Why it matters: wealth managers are valued on their client assets, so the buyers are betting those assets stay and keep growing.

### 3. MGX returns for Isomorphic Labs' $5 billion round

Isomorphic Labs, the Alphabet-backed AI drug-discovery company, raised a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia. All of its existing investors came back, Abu Dhabi's MGX among them.

The figure: $5 billion, six months after a $2.1 billion round in May.

Why it matters: none of its drug candidates has reached clinical trials yet. Investors are paying for the platform before any human data, and GCC life-science funds face the same call.
