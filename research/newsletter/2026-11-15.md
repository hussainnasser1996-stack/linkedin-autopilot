# Anthropic gets a price, and Saudi startups find lenders

Three Gulf deals from 8 to 15 November, and the dates I'm watching next week.

## The week in Gulf deals

### 1. Gulf holders of Anthropic are about to see a market price

Anthropic set its IPO range at $70 to $78 a share. At the top, that values it at about $1.4 trillion. Pricing is expected on 18 November and trading on Nasdaq from 19 November.

The figure: Qatar Investment Authority came in during the Series F in September 2025, at a $183 billion valuation.

Why it matters: QIA and MGX (which co-led the $380 billion Series G) bought in privately. Their stake sizes aren't public, but next week their entry prices get a public reference.

### 2. DIP Holding lists in Dubai

Dubai Investments sold 20% of DIP Holding at AED 1.12 a share, raising about AED 1.94 billion ($529 million). The shares rose as much as 6.2% on their first day, 11 November.

The figure: orders came in at about 4 times the shares on offer.

Why it matters: Bloomberg reported PIF and a Blackstone unit among the buyers, so a Saudi fund was reportedly in the book of a Dubai listing.

### 3. Saudi startups borrow where equity is thinner

SVC invested in Jadwa Investment's private credit fund. The fund has already put more than SAR 400 million into five companies, with a sixth expected this quarter.

The figure: Raqamyah says it has arranged up to $107 million of debt this year, double its 2025 volume.

Why it matters: Saudi VC came to $1 billion in the first nine months, down nearly a third. Debt gives founders with steady revenue another option. It only works if that revenue can carry the repayments.
